OpenAI Shuts Down Sora — Redirects GPUs to "Higher-Priority" Products

OpenAI has killed Sora, its AI video generation tool, just six months after its public launch. The Wall Street Journal reports that the product was consuming significant GPU capacity with limited traction, and OpenAI decided to redirect that compute toward its core model and product roadmap. TechCrunch's deep dive suggests the real reason goes beyond economics — Sora struggled with content moderation, quality consistency, and finding a paying audience willing to stick around. This is the clearest signal yet that in the GPU-constrained AI era, product lines compete for compute, not just users.

🏦 Aspire Relevance
A lesson in AI product portfolio management: flashy capabilities ≠ product-market fit. When GPU costs dominate your P&L, every product needs to justify its compute allocation. Worth noting as Aspire evaluates which AI capabilities to build vs. buy.

Mistral Loads Up: $830M Debt Deal for 13,800 Nvidia Chips & European AI Data Centers

France's Mistral has secured $830 million in debt financing from a seven-bank consortium — its first major debt raise — to purchase 13,800 Nvidia GPUs and build out a major data center near Paris. The company is targeting 200 megawatts of compute capacity across Europe by end of 2027, with expansion planned into Sweden. This is no longer just a model company play; Mistral is building European AI sovereignty from the silicon up. The fact that lenders are underwriting AI infrastructure debt signals the market now views GPU farms as bankable assets, not speculative bets.

DeepSeek Suffers Longest Outage Since Breakout — 7+ Hours Down

China's DeepSeek chatbot went dark for over seven hours on Monday — its longest outage since its viral 2025 debut. The major outage disrupted both consumer users and developers building on DeepSeek's APIs, arriving at the worst possible moment: just as the market awaits DeepSeek's next major model release. For a lab that built its reputation on fast iteration and benchmark competition with Western players, this is a credibility test. AI labs are graduating from hype cycles into uptime expectations, and outages now carry strategic consequences.

Regulation

White House Releases National AI Policy Framework — Legislative Recommendations for Congress

The White House released its "National Policy Framework for Artificial Intelligence" on March 20, now being analyzed by law firms and policy groups. The framework outlines legislative recommendations covering child safety protections for AI services, worker impacts, content provenance, and sector-specific guidelines. DLA Piper's analysis highlights the emphasis on children and parents — AI platforms "must take measures to protect children." This is the closest the U.S. has come to a structured federal AI regulatory blueprint under the current administration.
