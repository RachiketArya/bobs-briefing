OpenAI Closes $122B Round at $852B Valuation — Largest Private Deal in History

OpenAI has closed a deal that dwarfs every previous private funding round, IPO, and most acquisitions in history. Amazon invested $50 billion (with $35B contingent on IPO or AGI milestones), while Nvidia and SoftBank each put in $30 billion. Other backers include a16z, Abu Dhabi's MGX, D.E. Shaw, TPG, and T. Rowe Price. The company is now generating $2 billion per month in revenue, with enterprise sales at 40% and growing to 50% by year-end. CFO Sarah Friar says this "blows out of the water even the largest IPO that's ever been done." OpenAI has committed to spending over $1.4 trillion on physical AI infrastructure in coming years.

This concentration of capital reshapes the AI vendor landscape. With OpenAI now deeply tied to Amazon's cloud infrastructure, enterprise fintech customers face strategic lock-in decisions. Global VC Hits Record $297B in Q1 — AI Startups Capture 81% of All Funding

Crunchbase data confirms a staggering Q1 2026: global venture capital investment hit $297 billion, up 150% year-over-year. Regulation

CFTC Launches Innovation Task Force — AI & Autonomous Systems Get Regulatory Framework

The Commodity Futures Trading Commission has launched an Innovation Task Force to develop clear regulatory frameworks for three areas: crypto/blockchain, AI and autonomous systems, and prediction markets. Chairman Michael Selig says the goal is to "foster responsible innovation at home" while preventing American market participants from being "left on the sidelines." The task force will coordinate with the SEC's Crypto Task Force. This is one of the first explicit U.S. regulatory moves targeting AI agents in financial markets.

🏦 Aspire Relevance
Directly relevant to AI governance in fintech. The CFTC framing AI and autonomous systems alongside crypto signals that regulators view AI agents as a distinct category requiring oversight. If Aspire deploys any AI-driven trading, risk scoring, or automated compliance tools, this framework will matter. Worth monitoring the task force's output.
