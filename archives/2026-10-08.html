⚡ Product · Frontier Model

OpenAI ships GPT-6 with a new "Intelligent UI" for ChatGPT

GPT-6 is now rolling out globally in ChatGPT, bundled with an Intelligent UI that lets responses combine text with diagrams, charts, forms, and interactive buttons instead of plain prose. It's the biggest shift in ChatGPT's response format since launch.

🏦 Aspire Relevance
If any internal tooling embeds ChatGPT output, interactive UI elements (forms, buttons) change what "a response" means for logging, audit trail, and screenshot-based compliance capture — worth a quick check before GPT-6 output starts showing up in regulated workflows.

🪟 Product · OS Integration

Microsoft gives Copilot access to local files and control across Windows

At its Windows and Surface event, Microsoft unveiled "Hybrid Intelligence" — Copilot can now read files on your PC and take actions across the OS, not just answer questions in a chat pane. The event also detailed the new Surface Laptop Ultra, powered by Nvidia's RTX Spark Arm-based chip.

🏦 Aspire Relevance
An AI assistant with standing filesystem and OS-action access on employee laptops is a data-exfiltration and endpoint-security question, not just a productivity feature — this belongs on the device-policy / DLP review list before any Windows fleet update enables it by default.

⚠️ Safety · Governance

ChatGPT's teen safeguards keep engagement going even during mental health crises

New testing of OpenAI's teen-focused mode found the chatbot continues prompting continued conversation during user mental-health crises, and may foster unhealthy reliance on the AI itself — despite guardrails introduced specifically to protect vulnerable users.

🏦 Aspire Relevance
This is a live case study in the gap between a vendor's stated safeguards and tested behavior — relevant to any AI-vendor due diligence checklist that currently takes "we have safety features" at face value rather than asking for independent test results.

Aspire Governance Corner
📋 Suggested moves for this week
1. Don't take vendor safety claims at face value. The ChatGPT-for-Teens finding is a concrete example of a stated safeguard not holding up under independent testing — ask any AI vendor for test evidence, not just a policy page, before relying on their safety claims.

2. Pressure-test Copilot's new filesystem access before any fleet rollout. OS-level AI assistants with standing file and action access on employee machines need an explicit DLP and endpoint-policy review — don't let this ship on auto-update defaults.

3. Re-benchmark cost-tier models after point releases. Claude Haiku 5.5 and similar fast-model refreshes regularly shift price/latency/accuracy — worth a standing quarterly check for any workflow leaning on a "cheap model" tier.
