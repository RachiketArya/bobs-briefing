AI has delivered "zero financial payoff" outside tech, Apollo economist says

Despite massive corporate investment and hype, Apollo's chief economist argues AI has produced no measurable financial payoff for corporate America outside the tech sector itself — a direct challenge to the ROI narrative enterprises have been selling internally.

🏦 Aspire Relevance
Good ammunition for a sober budget conversation: before the next AI tooling renewal, Aspire should be able to show a measured productivity or cost delta, not just adoption numbers. This is the kind of external data point a finance partner will bring up unprompted.

🏛️ Industry · Governance

r/opensource bans AI-made, AI-reliant, and AI-targeted projects outright

The subreddit's moderators posted new submission rules banning any project that uses AI, is built by AI, or is intended for AI — part of a growing backlash in open-source communities against AI-generated contributions and tooling.

🏦 Aspire Relevance
A visible signal of community pushback against unlabeled AI-generated code and content. If Aspire sources any open-source components, worth checking maintainer policies on AI contributions — provenance and license risk are starting to track together.
