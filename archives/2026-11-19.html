🏛️ Regulation · Fintech

Citi tells customers: if your AI agent gets hacked, that's on you

Citi is the latest major bank to formally put the brakes on personal finance AI agents, joining peers in making clear that liability for agent-driven fraud or error sits with the customer, not the institution.

🏦 Aspire Relevance
This is the liability-allocation pattern Aspire needs baked into any customer-facing agentic feature now, not after an incident. If banks won't indemnify agent actions on their own rails, Aspire's own agent-initiated transactions need equally explicit terms, step-up auth on money-moving actions, and an audit trail that survives a dispute.
