Apple's new "Reference Image" feature tries to prove an iPhone photo wasn't AI-edited

🧪 Research

OpenAI claims a solution to the 90-year-old Navier-Stokes problem

OpenAI says it found a solution to the Navier-Stokes Millennium Prize Problem — the fluid-dynamics equation that has resisted proof for roughly 90 years — publishing a full writeup and a formal proof encoded in the Lean theorem prover. The claim is drawing scrutiny from mathematicians before it's accepted as settled.

🏦 Aspire Relevance
Less directly fintech-relevant, but a signal worth tracking: if AI-assisted formal proof (Lean) is now credible at Millennium Prize difficulty, the same toolchain applies to formally verifying financial models and smart-contract logic — an emerging assurance category to watch.

Aspire Governance Corner
📋 Suggested moves for this week
1. Add a "safety-committee composition" line item to AI vendor due diligence. With named alignment experts now sitting on lab governance boards (Christiano at OpenAI), ask each AI vendor who sits on their safety/security committee and what authority it actually has — treat it as a real control, not a PR page.
