Amazon plans a "World Product Graph" to index all online shopping data

Amazon is reportedly building a vault of product data covering everything sold anywhere online — even non-Amazon products — and will surface it through its own site, per Reuters. The goal: make Amazon's own search less dependent on sellers' own listings.

A reminder that the "AI search/agent layer" land grab isn't limited to chatbots — anyone with transaction or catalog data is racing to own the discovery layer. Relevant if Aspire thinks about partner data moats.
