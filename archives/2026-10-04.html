🔒 Security · Culture

OpenAI safety employee resigns, says the company's "culture is broken"

David Robinson, who used to write the safety reports attached to OpenAI's major model releases, resigned this week and published an editorial in The Atlantic calling out what he describes as a broken safety culture inside the company.

🏦 Aspire Relevance
A named insider publicly disputing a frontier lab's safety process is the kind of vendor-risk signal that belongs in diligence notes before any deeper OpenAI dependency — don't wait for a regulator to flag it first.
Source → TechCrunch
🖥️ Security · Platform Controls

Apple will restrict Mac disk access because AI agents "substantially" raise the risk

Apple confirmed new limits on full disk access permissions for macOS apps, saying explicitly that increasingly capable AI agents change the risk calculus for any app with broad access to a user's files, Mail, and browsing history.

🏦 Aspire Relevance
A major platform vendor now treating "AI agent with broad filesystem access" as its own threat tier is worth folding into endpoint policy review — any assistant tooling with Mac disk access (Adam included) should be re-checked against the new permission model once it ships, not grandfathered.
Source → The Verge
🏗️ Industry · Infrastructure Politics

Amazon claims it no longer uses NDAs to quiet data-center backlash

AWS's CEO pushed back on "widespread suspicion" around AI data centers, telling communities the company has stopped using non-disclosure agreements with locals affected by new sites — an unusually direct damage-control move from a hyperscaler under public pressure.

🏦 Aspire Relevance
A core cloud vendor publicly defending its community-relations practices signals real friction in AI infrastructure buildout — worth a line in any multi-year compute capacity or vendor-concentration assumption, not just a PR footnote.
Source → TechCrunch
💼 Product · Fintech Case Study

Chatham Financial cuts trade validation from 30 minutes to under 4 using Codex and GPT-5.6

Chatham Financial, a capital-markets risk advisory, rebuilt internal workflows around Codex and GPT-5.6, cutting trade validation from 30 minutes down to under 4 minutes, per OpenAI's published case study.

OpenAI says it disrupted a coordinated campaign to distill its model's reasoning

OpenAI published details of a campaign it says attempted to systematically extract protected model reasoning through adversarial distillation, and the defenses it has since strengthened against this class of attack.

🏦 Aspire Relevance
