A Leading VC's Playbook for AI Agents — And Why Context Is King

James Wang (Weighty Thoughts / ex-Bridgewater, Google[x]) published a comprehensive guest post on AI Made Simple detailing how he runs AI agents for daily briefings, meeting transcription, CRM management, and research. The key insight: agents become powerful only with rich context (detailed CLAUDE.md instructions, curated document databases, iterative refinement). He processed 8.5 hours of investment meetings into a 30-page report in hours — work that used to take weeks. He also warns against reckless setups like OpenClaw without proper sandboxing.

🏦 Aspire Relevance
This mirrors exactly the kind of agentic workflow Aspire could deploy for compliance review, customer analysis, or internal ops. Wang's security-first approach (sandboxed MCPs, no arbitrary web access, denied bash execution) is a solid template for fintech agent governance.
