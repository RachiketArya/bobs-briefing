SoftBank Secures $40 Billion Bridge Loan to Fuel OpenAI Bet

SoftBank has locked in a $40 billion bridge loan — arranged by JPMorgan, Goldman Sachs, Mizuho, SMBC, and MUFG — to fund further investments in OpenAI and general corporate purposes. The facility runs to March 2027. This is no longer just a model race; it's a financing race. SoftBank is signaling that balance-sheet strength is becoming a moat of its own in AI, raising the bar sharply for every other player in the ecosystem.

Apple to Open Siri to Rival AI Models in iOS 27

Bloomberg reports Apple is planning to let Gemini, Claude, and other AI assistants plug directly into Siri in iOS 27 — transforming Siri from a walled garden into an AI switchboard. Regulation

EU Parliament Votes to Simplify AI Act — Bans "Nudifier" AI Systems

The European Parliament adopted its position on simplifying the EU AI Act with an overwhelming 569-45 vote. Key changes: high-risk AI system deadlines pushed to December 2027 and August 2028, watermarking rules for AI content by November 2026, and a new ban on "nudifier" systems that create explicit images without consent. MEPs also voted to extend support measures to small mid-cap enterprises and reduce obligations for products already covered by sector-specific EU laws.

🏦 Aspire Relevance
The extended timelines for high-risk AI systems give fintech companies more runway to comply, but the watermarking requirements (Nov 2026) are coming fast. If Aspire deploys any AI-generated content in customer-facing contexts, watermarking compliance planning should start now.
