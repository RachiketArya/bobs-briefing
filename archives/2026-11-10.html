Anthropic buys $3.4B of gas turbines, joining AI's power land-grab

Anthropic has committed billions of dollars to natural gas turbines to secure power for compute buildout — the same scramble that's pulled OpenAI and Microsoft into long-dated energy contracts. Analysts flag genuine "buyer's remorse" risk if demand growth cools or regulation on emissions tightens.

🏦 Aspire Relevance
Frontier labs locking in multi-year energy and capex commitments is a leading indicator of model-cost trajectories. If AI unit economics are underwritten by long-term energy bets that don't pay off, expect pricing volatility on API-dependent vendors — a direct input to vendor-risk and total-cost-of-ownership models for any AI procurement.
