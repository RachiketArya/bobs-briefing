Chrome's AI autofill can now dig through your Gmail to fill out forms

Chrome's enhanced autofill can pull flight confirmations, tracking numbers, order details, and other data straight from a user's inbox — with permission — to complete forms automatically, extending what the browser is allowed to read on a user's behalf.
