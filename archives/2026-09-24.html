🔬 Research · Frontier Science

Anthropic's Claude autonomously discovers a CRISPR-like enzyme system

Anthropic says Claude, running in its newly launched wet lab, autonomously identified a novel enzyme system with repeat-based machinery similar to what underlies CRISPR gene editing — the first tangible output from pairing frontier models with physical lab equipment rather than just literature review.

🏦 Aspire Relevance
Model vendors are moving from "assistant" to "autonomous discovery agent" with physical-world actions. For any vendor-risk framework, this is the shape of what "agentic AI" will mean in 12–18 months — plan governance for models that take real-world actions, not just generate text.

🏛️ Regulation · US Congress

Sanders and Casar introduce a bill to ban "superintelligence" development, with prison terms for violators

Sen. Bernie Sanders and Rep. Greg Casar filed legislation defining artificial superintelligence as tech capable of "destruction or disempowerment of humanity" and proposing to criminalize its development. It's unlikely to pass this Congress, but it puts a hard regulatory marker down right as Anthropic publicizes autonomous-discovery results.

Symbolic today, but it signals where US AI-safety politics could head. Track this alongside EU AI Act enforcement — a shift from "disclosure" regimes to "capability bans" would be a materially different compliance posture to prepare for.

💰 Funding · Biotech

Enveda raises $311M to push AI-derived, nature-based drugs into clinical trials

The round values the AI drug-discovery biotech at $2 billion. Enveda mines natural compounds with AI models and is currently testing candidates for skin conditions and for preserving weight loss after patients stop GLP-1 drugs — a real commercial angle riding the Ozempic wave.

A concrete data point for anyone modeling AI-native biotech valuations or evaluating AI-in-healthcare vendors — discovery speed is becoming a fundable moat on its own, separate from clinical outcomes.
Source: TechCrunch →
⚡ Product · Consumer AI

ChatGPT's mobile app gets voice-driven agentic tasks for paid users

Pro and Plus subscribers can now use the "Work" tab on the ChatGPT mobile app to trigger and monitor agentic tasks by voice, extending OpenAI's agent push from desktop/browser into the pocket — the same direction Meta and Google are pushing consumer agents.

Employee-owned mobile AI agents acting on tasks (email, bookings, purchases) raise the same shadow-IT and data-leakage questions BYOD did a decade ago. Worth a line in any AI acceptable-use policy refresh.

🏛️ Regulation · Infrastructure

California moves to force disclosure of data-center power and water use

Gov. Newsom signed bills that will give communities visibility into how AI data centers affect local electricity bills and water supply — the first US state move to formally attach transparency obligations to the AI infrastructure boom rather than just the models themselves.

If ESG/sustainability reporting extends to AI-vendor infrastructure footprints, this is the template regulators reach for next — worth flagging to anyone doing vendor due diligence on cloud/AI providers with US operations.
Source: The Verge →
