General Intuition, which trains foundation models to give agents spatial and temporal reasoning for robotics, is in talks to raise at a $6B pre-money valuation from Valor Ventures, Point72 Ventures, and Seven Seven Six — a sign capital is rotating from pure chat models toward embodied/agentic AI.

🔐 Security · Privacy

Instinct's powerful AI assistant is raising privacy and security concerns

Early testers say Instinct's assistant is remarkably capable, but its sweeping account access, broad terms of service, and ability to act on users' behalf are drawing scrutiny over exactly what it's allowed to do unsupervised.

🏦 Aspire Relevance
Directly relevant to any "agentic assistant" pilot at Aspire: before granting an AI agent standing access to email, calendars, or systems, map exactly what scopes it holds and what it's permitted to act on without a human in the loop — write that into the vendor-risk questionnaire now, before adoption outpaces governance.

🛠️ Product

OpenAI is building AI agents for everything. Will everyone use them?

OpenAI is pushing its agent tooling well beyond software engineers, aiming to bring general-purpose AI agents to mainstream, non-technical users — the frontier lab's biggest bet yet on agents as the default interface.

As agent tooling gets pushed to non-technical staff, the operational risk shifts from "can it do the task" to "will an untrained employee grant it too much authority." Pair any internal agent rollout with a lightweight approval tier (what an agent can do unsupervised vs. what needs sign-off).

🧪 Research · Speculation

Who's behind the new "stealth model" Ox Alpha?

A previously unknown AI model called Ox Alpha has appeared on evaluation leaderboards and social feeds with strong benchmark performance and no confirmed lab behind it, triggering a wave of speculation across AI Twitter/X about its origin.

🏦 Aspire Relevance
Unattributed models with no known provenance are not something to route production traffic through, however good the benchmarks look — treat "stealth model" leaderboard entries as entertainment until a lab claims it and publishes terms, data-handling, and support commitments.
