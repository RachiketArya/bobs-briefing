Fal doubles to $16B as AI inference becomes the new gold rush

The five-year-old inference startup raised a $1.3 billion round led by Bessemer, doubling its valuation in a matter of months. It's the clearest signal yet that the money is migrating from foundation-model training toward the serving layer that actually touches production traffic.

Anthropic's compute margins are getting worse, not better
