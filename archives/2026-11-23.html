🏛️ Regulation · Policy

UN diplomats fail to agree on any rules for autonomous weapons, kick the can to "options discussions"

A week of Geneva meetings meant to produce binding rules on "killer robots" — autonomous weapons systems — ended with representatives agreeing only to keep talking. No definitions, no red lines, no enforcement mechanism. The gap between how fast autonomous weapons capability is moving and how slow the governance process is moving just got more visible.

🏦 Aspire Relevance: Low direct exposure, but it's a useful data point on how far international AI governance lags capability generally — a reminder not to assume regulatory clarity will arrive before Aspire needs to make its own AI-use policy calls.
