💰 Funding · IPO

All 7 Anthropic founders set to cash out ~$250M each in the IPO

Each of Anthropic's seven co-founders plans to sell the identical number of shares in the IPO, netting roughly $250M apiece at the midpoint price while the group keeps control of the company. It's a rare, deliberately symmetrical cash-out structure for a frontier lab going public.

🏦 Aspire Relevance
A frontier model vendor's governance structure through an IPO matters for continuity risk — founder liquidity events can shift incentives and board composition even when control is formally retained. Worth a line in the vendor-concentration file if Anthropic is a material dependency.

🤝 Acquisition · Health

Oura buys AI-native primary care startup Counsel Health for $625M

The wearables maker is paying $625M for an AI-native primary care platform, just weeks after shelving its own up-to-$2.2B IPO over "uncertainty in the IPO market." Buying growth via M&A while deferring a public listing is a notable sequencing choice.

A company pausing its own IPO to instead fund a $625M acquisition is a live data point on how AI-adjacent firms are managing capital allocation amid choppy public markets — relevant context if Aspire is modeling IPO-market sentiment for AI-sector counterparties.

⚡ Product · Enterprise

Anthropic launches its own AI-powered office suite

Anthropic, long known for enterprise coding tools, is now shipping its own office productivity suite — following the same playbook that pushed OpenAI, Meta, and others into adjacent office-software territory after Claude Code's success.

🏦 Aspire Relevance
Frontier labs bundling office productivity with their model APIs raises real vendor-lock-in questions for any org already standardized on Claude for coding — a single-vendor office + AI stack concentrates risk and makes exit costs higher. Worth flagging before any wider Claude rollout.

🛡️ Security · Platform Policy

eBay appears to have shut the door on third-party AI shopping agents

eBay has blocked AI agents — including Meta's Muse — from browsing its platform, the latest marketplace to push back against agentic shopping bots scraping listings on behalf of users. A reader reported their Muse agent's weeks-long watch-curation workflow simply stopped working.

🏦 Aspire Relevance
Any internal agent workflow that depends on scraping or browsing third-party platforms carries real continuity risk — a platform's unilateral ToS enforcement can break a production pipeline overnight with no warning. Build dependency maps for agent workflows that touch external sites you don't control.

⚡ Product · Models

Google rolls out Gemini 4 Carbon with an agent "swarm" feature

Google has opened Gemini 4 Carbon to the general public after a two-week preview with "cybersecurity defenders." Google is pitching Carbon as its strongest model yet, built to compete directly with Anthropic and OpenAI's latest, and highlighting a multi-agent "swarm" capability for coordinated task execution.

🏦 Aspire Relevance
A third credible frontier model entrant strengthens the case for a multi-vendor model strategy rather than single-provider lock-in — worth a quick capability/cost benchmark against current Claude/GPT workloads before any renewal decision.
