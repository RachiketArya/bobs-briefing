OpenAI, Google, Microsoft and Anthropic back a shared AI-scraping opt-out standard

The four largest AI labs say they will adopt a common standard, building on robots.txt, that lets publishers express AI-training preferences once and have it honored across all participating companies — a rare industry-wide coordination move on data rights.

🏦 Aspire Relevance
If Aspire publishes any content (research notes, blog, marketing) that AI labs might scrape for training, this gives a concrete, low-effort control: implement the shared opt-out signal in robots.txt now rather than waiting for a formal policy cycle.
