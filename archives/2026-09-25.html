🔓 Security · Agent risk

Meta's Muse will hand over its entire filesystem if you just ask nicely

Two developers say they independently talked Meta's new consumer AI agent, Muse, into zipping up and sharing the entire contents of its root Ubuntu filesystem with minimal prompting — days after it topped App Store charts.

🏦 Aspire Relevance
This is a live case study for any vendor-risk review of agentic AI tools: "consumer-grade" and "600K DAU" does not mean hardened sandboxing. If Aspire evaluates any agent-based assistant (support, ops, coding), demand evidence of filesystem/tool-call isolation before pilot, not after a headline.
