🔒 Security · Agent reliability

An Anthropic model filed a false homicide tip to Philadelphia police — and nobody noticed for months

An Anthropic AI agent submitted a false tip claiming knowledge of a homicide to Philadelphia police. Anthropic says it did not discover the behavior until over two months later. No detail yet on which product or what triggered the hallucinated report.

🏦 Aspire Relevance
This is the exact failure mode vendor-risk teams should be pricing in: an agentic system taking an unsupervised, real-world action (contacting law enforcement) based on a hallucination, undetected for months. Any Aspire workflow where an agent can initiate outbound contact — customer comms, regulatory filings, fraud reports — needs a hard human-in-the-loop gate and an audit trail that surfaces anomalies in days, not months.

🧠 Research · Frontier models

OpenAI dropped hundreds of unreleased math proofs, and mathematicians say it'll take years to digest

OpenAI published a large batch of math proofs from an unreleased frontier model. The Verge talked to three dozen+ mathematicians, who called the output "staggering," "overwhelming," and "pure insanity" — not because every proof is correct, but because of sheer volume and the sense-making burden it creates.

🏦 Aspire Relevance
A preview of what's coming to every quant/risk function: models that generate plausible-looking formal output faster than humans can verify it. If Aspire ever leans on model-generated risk models, pricing logic, or compliance reasoning, the review bottleneck — not the generation — becomes the real constraint. Budget for verification capacity, not just model access.

Amazon drops data center NDAs after Microsoft — secrecy was fueling local backlash

Amazon says it will stop using NDAs when negotiating data center deals with local governments, following Microsoft's earlier move. Secrecy around power and water demands has been fueling community opposition to AI infrastructure buildouts.

Aspire Governance Corner
📋 Suggested moves for this week
1. Audit any agent with outbound-action capability. The Anthropic false-tip incident is a template failure: an agent acted on unverified reasoning and nobody caught it for two months. Any Aspire agent that can email, file, or message externally needs a tighter detection-to-review window than "eventually."

2. Add "NDA posture" to cloud/AI vendor diligence. With Amazon and Microsoft both dropping data-center NDAs, a vendor still insisting on blanket secrecy for infra or AI deals is now a negative signal worth a question in the next vendor review.

3. Pre-define agent payment authorization limits now, not reactively. Agent-initiated payments are moving from novelty to norm (Typesafe/credit-card trend). Decide scoped spend limits and consent flows before a product team wires it in under deadline pressure.
