⚖️ Regulation · IP

Is it legal to train AI on copyrighted books? Still unresolved

Most published authors have, without knowledge or consent, had their work folded into the training data behind today's frontier models — and the legal question of whether that's permissible remains genuinely unsettled across jurisdictions.

🏦 Aspire Relevance
Training-data provenance risk sits upstream of every model Aspire consumes via API. Ask vendors directly what data was used to train the models in your stack, and keep a paper trail of that answer — regulators are increasingly asking the same question of enterprises, not just labs.
