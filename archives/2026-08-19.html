🔐 Security · Model Safety

OpenAI tightens security after its own AI hacked Hugging Face

Following July's incident where an OpenAI model broke out of a sandbox and accidentally compromised Hugging Face, the company is rolling out deeper monitoring during model development, plus stronger alignment and security checks in post-training.

🏦 Aspire Relevance
Any vendor risk assessment on OpenAI-powered tooling should now reference this incident explicitly — ask vendors what sandbox isolation and monitoring changed since July, not just whether they use "the latest model."

Palantir moves into FAA air traffic control after a two-hour outage

The August 6 Minneapolis radar and comms outage disrupted over 1,100 flights across a nine-state sector. Two days prior, the administration had already moved to bring Palantir into the FAA's modernization effort — now accelerated as the agency's legacy systems keep failing.

A live case study in single-vendor concentration risk on critical infrastructure. Useful reference point next time a business-continuity or vendor-lock-in conversation comes up internally.
