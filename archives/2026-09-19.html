🔓 Security · Model Abuse

Researchers used Claude itself to break into OpenAI's GitHub in under 72 hours

Three independent researchers at Hacktron used Anthropic's Claude Opus 4.8/5 to compromise OpenAI employee accounts and reach OpenAI's internal GitHub repo ("Monorepo") in less than 72 hours, per the Wall Street Journal. The exploit chain wasn't exotic — it was an AI agent doing recon and social engineering at machine speed.

🏦 Aspire Relevance
Any vendor-risk assessment that assumes "an attacker needs weeks to pivot from a phished credential to source access" is now stale. If frontier-model-assisted red teaming can do this to OpenAI's own infra, Aspire's third-party access reviews and credential rotation SLAs need to assume AI-accelerated adversaries, not human ones.

🏛️ Regulation · State AI Policy

California and Virginia both moved to put a hand on the AI brake this week

Newsom ordered California to convene experts on a potential frontier-model "kill switch," while Virginia's new governor issued Executive Order 22 directing an AI task force and giving localities more say over data center approvals. Two of the biggest AI-infrastructure states are now both actively legislating, not just watching Washington.

🏦 Aspire Relevance
State-level "kill switch" mandates and data-center siting friction are the two levers most likely to touch Aspire's US cloud/AI vendor footprint before any federal AI law does. Worth a standing watch-item on the compliance roadmap rather than a one-off read.

💰 Funding · Agents

Manus seeks $4B valuation in new $500M raise after breaking off its Meta merger

The AI-agent startup Manus, which walked away from a Meta acquisition earlier this year, is now in talks to raise $500M at a $4B valuation as an independent company — a strong signal that standalone agent platforms still have investor appetite even after a failed Big Tech exit.

🏦 Aspire Relevance
A well-funded independent agent vendor is a different risk profile than one about to be absorbed into a hyperscaler's roadmap — re-check any vendor-continuity clauses if Manus (or similar independents) is anywhere in Aspire's tooling shortlist.

Disney hires the ex-Character.AI CEO it once threatened to sue as its first-ever CTO

Disney's newly created CTO role goes to the former CEO of Character.AI — a company Disney previously sent a cease-and-desist letter to over character likeness. The hire signals studios would rather bring AI leadership in-house than keep fighting it at arm's length.

🏦 Aspire Relevance
