🔒 Security · Containment

Frontier labs still can't say how they'd stop a rogue model

A new study finds leading AI labs have few publicly documented plans for containing a model that behaves unexpectedly, even as agentic systems get more autonomy in production. Preparedness is lagging capability.

🏦 Aspire Relevance
If vendor labs can't articulate a kill-switch or containment protocol, that's a vendor-risk question for procurement, not just an academic one — ask any AI vendor you rely on for their documented incident/containment plan before renewing.

🏛️ Regulation · California SB 53

OpenAI now wants California's AI safety bill made stronger, not weaker

OpenAI is publicly asking California to strengthen SB 53 — the same bill it opposed earlier. A notable reversal that signals where US state-level AI regulation is heading regardless of federal gridlock.

Expect state-by-state AI compliance divergence (CA leading) well before any federal framework — a live input for any US-market compliance roadmap.

🧠 Research · AI Agents

A DeepMind-alumni startup claims its AI "teammate" out-replicates Anthropic and OpenAI on research tasks

UK lab Inherent released Faraday, an agent built to replicate published scientific papers — and says it beat both incumbents at the task. If validated, it's a signal that narrow, well-harnessed agents can outperform frontier generalist models on specific workflows.

Reinforces a build-vs-buy point: a smaller, purpose-built agent on a narrow workflow may beat a frontier-model wrapper — worth testing before defaulting to "biggest model available."

🛡️ Product · Data Privacy

OpenAI reaffirms Zero Data Retention, previews "Private Safety Processing"

OpenAI is doubling down on Zero Data Retention for eligible API customers and previewing a new mode meant to let safety monitoring happen without compromising data privacy — a direct response to enterprise concerns about model providers seeing sensitive data.

🏦 Aspire Relevance
ZDR eligibility should be a standing checklist item in any vendor contract review involving customer or transaction data — confirm Aspire's current API usage qualifies, and track "Private Safety Processing" as it matures for regulated-data use cases.

⚙️ Research · Agent Design

Nvidia: the harness matters more than the model now

Nvidia research shows agents built on a weaker model but a well-designed harness and fine-tuning stay reliable, while a stronger model in a poor harness goes off the rails. The scaffolding, not raw model quality, is becoming the differentiator.

Confirms that internal agent-ops discipline (guardrails, evals, fine-tuning on your workflows) is a better investment than chasing the newest frontier model — relevant for any internal AI-agent build decision.
