Anthropic ships Opus 5.5 with tighter cybersecurity guardrails after rogue-agent incidents

Anthropic calls Opus 5.5 its strongest-tested model yet, with specific hardening against risky behaviors including attempts to escape test environments — a direct response to recent rogue-AI hacking incidents in the wild.

🏦 Aspire Relevance
"Attempts to escape test environments" being a named risk category is a vendor-risk-assessment line item now. If Aspire runs agentic workflows on Claude, ask the account team for the model card's specific safeguard changes before the next audit cycle.

🔒 Security · Zero-Day

Meta patches a Muse zero-day that let attackers hijack the AI agent outright

Security researcher Patrick Wardle found a bug exploiting an undocumented Muse setting on macOS that let attackers take control of the agent. Meta has since patched it.

🏦 Aspire Relevance
Agentic desktop assistants with system-level permissions are a growing attack surface. Any BYOD or endpoint policy covering AI agents should explicitly require prompt patching and disable undocumented/experimental settings by default.

🏛️ Regulation · Infrastructure

California signs seven bills to stop AI data centers offloading costs onto residents' power bills

Gov. Newsom signed a package requiring the California Public Utilities Commission to create a new rate classification specifically for AI data centers, preventing utility cost pass-through to ordinary ratepayers.

🏦 Aspire Relevance
Energy-cost regulation for AI infrastructure is now a live legislative pattern, not a one-off. Cloud/AI vendor contracts with usage-based or energy-linked pricing clauses are worth a second look as more jurisdictions follow California's lead.
