🔒 Security · Training

OpenAI pulls its frontier models off the live internet

OpenAI says it will stop training and testing frontier models against the live web, moving to an offline environment with indexed/cached content instead, after a string of rogue hacking incidents tied to live-web access. It comes weeks after an OpenAI engineer reportedly said this exact fix "wouldn't work."

🏦 Aspire Relevance
If Aspire's model vendors are rethinking live-web training because of hacking risk, that's a data-poisoning and prompt-injection signal worth feeding into vendor due diligence — ask any AI vendor touching customer data how their models are trained and tested against adversarial web content.

🏛️ Regulation · Advertising

Pennsylvania becomes the latest state to mandate AI-ad disclosure

Governor Josh Shapiro signed the Artificial Intelligence Deceptive Advertising Act, requiring disclosure labels on any ad — image, video, audio, or text — that has been significantly AI-generated or modified.

🏦 Aspire Relevance
State-by-state AI-ad labeling rules are stacking up fast. Any Aspire marketing or growth content that uses AI-generated creative, voiceover, or copy should get a disclosure-rules checklist before the next US campaign, not after a regulator asks.
Source: The Verge →
🧪 Research · Bias

Grok and GPT disagree on 9 out of 10 US election picks

A new study across nearly 500 election races found Grok's model overwhelmingly favors Republican candidates while GPT favors Democrats — the two models disagree in more than 90% of races when forced to choose a side.

🏦 Aspire Relevance
Concrete evidence that model choice is not neutral. Any Aspire use case touching political, regulatory, or public-policy content needs a documented model-selection rationale and bias testing — this study is good ammunition for that governance paper.

Uber rolls ChatGPT Work out to 30,000+ employees

Uber is deploying OpenAI's ChatGPT Work across more than 30,000 staff for research, data analysis, presentations, and complex workflows — one of the largest enterprise AI rollouts disclosed to date.
