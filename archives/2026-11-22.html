Anthropic's $81B Nasdaq debut lands the same week AI stocks enter a correction

Anthropic went public with a headline-grabbing $81B valuation, but the listing coincided with a broader pullback in AI stocks, Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang's resignation amid a scandal, and Nvidia halting H200 sales into China. The Verge's AI team framed it as a test of whether a "safety-first" lab can stay disciplined once it's a public company answering to markets.

🧮 Research · Mathematics

An Anthropic mathematician says Claude helped crack the Riemann Hypothesis — a rival human proof surfaced the same week

Levent Alpöge claims a Claude-assisted proof of the 167-year-old Riemann Hypothesis, one of math's six unsolved Millennium Prize problems. A separate, independently verified Lean-formalized proof also emerged from the mathlib community the same week, raising real questions about priority and whether AI materially sped up the breakthrough or merely assisted drafting.

🏦 Aspire Relevance
Competing claims (AI-assisted vs. human-verified formal proof) is exactly the kind of provenance dispute Aspire should expect more of as AI co-authorship spreads — a preview of attribution and audit-trail questions that will eventually hit AI-assisted financial models and risk calculations too.

Nvidia halts H200 sales to China while still absorbing fallout from Jensen Huang's ouster

Nvidia posted healthy earnings growth this week but confirmed it will stop selling H200 chips into China, even as the company works through the leadership shock of CEO Jensen Huang's resignation amid scandal. 🔒 Security · Privacy

Signal starts blocking Gemini and Siri from reading your screen

Signal is restricting screenshot capability on Android and iOS specifically to stop OS-level agentic AI features (Gemini, Siri) from reading message content off-screen. It's the first major consumer app to actively defend against agentic AI "screen understanding" as a privacy threat rather than a convenience feature.

🏦 Aspire Relevance
If Aspire deploys any on-device or OS-level agentic assistants internally, this is a concrete precedent for a "no-screen-read" exception list for sensitive apps (compliance chat, client comms) — worth a line in the AI acceptable-use policy now, before an incident forces it.

Trump threatens a "tremendous tax" on companies that build AI data centers outside the US

President Trump said companies moving AI data center investment offshore would face a steep tax, even as US states and cities simultaneously push back on new local data center applications — a regulatory squeeze from both directions on where AI infrastructure capital actually lands.
