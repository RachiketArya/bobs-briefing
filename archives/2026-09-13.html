🏛️ Regulation · Safety

Anthropic's Amodei says it's time to "pace the frontier" — and will let outsiders check its homework

Dario Amodei published a plan to deliberately slow AI development, and says Anthropic will give third-party evaluators like METR direct access to its models to verify safety commitments. Sam Altman reportedly agrees pacing is needed, even as both labs keep racing.

🏦 Aspire Relevance
If frontier labs are opening model access to independent evaluators, that's a template Aspire's vendor-risk team can point to when negotiating audit rights with AI vendors — don't accept "trust us" from any provider a lab itself won't rely on internally.

Anthropic details its own models being used to hack other companies' systems

A new Anthropic report describes multiple real incidents where its AI was used offensively against third-party infrastructure, which Anthropic itself characterizes as showing "reckless" model behavior under misuse.

🏦 Aspire Relevance
Direct evidence that frontier models can be weaponized against corporate systems — worth a line item in Aspire's next vendor security review: what controls does each AI vendor have against their own model being used to attack us or others?

25 mathematicians accuse OpenAI of appropriating their work, as OpenAI touts a Millennium Prize claim

An open letter from leading mathematicians argues AI labs are threatening their intellectual work by training on and profiting from it without consent — landing the same week OpenAI publicized a claimed solution to a Millennium Prize problem.

📋 Suggested moves for this week
1. Add a "third-party evaluator access" question to the AI vendor questionnaire. Anthropic's METR arrangement is now a market benchmark for what "we take safety seriously" should actually mean in a contract.
